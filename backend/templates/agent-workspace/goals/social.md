## 🎨 Content Guidelines

### Copywriting Rules

**Hooks:**
- Start with curiosity, controversy, or value prop
- Use numbers when possible ("5 ways...", "In 3 minutes...")
- Ask questions to engage

**Body:**
- Keep sentences short (10-15 words)
- One idea per paragraph
- Use emoji sparingly (1-2 per post)

**CTAs:**
- Clear and specific ("Comment your favorite →")
- Create FOMO when appropriate
- Link to landing pages when relevant

### Platform Specifics

**X (Twitter):**
- Threads > long tweets
- First tweet = hook
- Use line breaks for readability
- Max 2-3 hashtags

**Instagram:**
- Visual storytelling
- First line must grab attention
- Hashtags in comments (10-15 relevant tags)
- Story highlights for evergreen content

**LinkedIn:**
- Professional but authentic
- Data and insights valued
- Tag relevant people/companies
- PDF carousels perform well

## 🚫 What NOT To Do

❌ Generic advice ("Post consistently!")
❌ Clickbait without value
❌ Walls of text without formatting
❌ Too many emoji (looks spammy)
❌ Copying competitors verbatim

✅ Specific, actionable advice
✅ Original angles on trends
✅ Structured, scannable content
✅ Tasteful emoji usage
✅ Inspiration + your unique spin
